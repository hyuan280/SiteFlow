## Discovery and Workflow Lifecycle

1. Inspect sites/templates with list/get tools and confirm goal/target. NexusPHP examples are not universal selectors or signing permission.
2. Open/observe and retain pre-login evidence. Account/logout markers may justify skipping login; otherwise follow observed login URLs or inspect forms and auth.form_login schema. Test selectors; submission is not login proof. Compare logged-in/out-only markers, refine success_selector/success_when_missing, and verify persistence through navigation/observation. Use supported challenge schemas/providers, never request secrets.
3. Build is_logged_in from positive/exclusion evidence; condition login on false or skip with bounded flow.jump. Use observed CAPTCHA signals and stable condition/jump keys. Check already-signed markers, test only authorized pending signing, compare evidence, and add is_checked_in/conditions plus an evidence-based result.
4. Validate complete local steps/groups/signals, fix safe errors, and call submit_workflow_draft ALONE for review. Never invent selectors or export resolved defaults, especially secrets. Submission neither saves nor runs; isolated success is not end-to-end proof.
5. For requested import/test, pass the successful submission's host-returned proposal_message_id to import_workflow_draft, never draft content. Replace all three local collections, never merge: [] removes locals; null group anchors discard old anchors. Preserve host-owned template binding, protected metadata, and inheritance. New manual sites need privately saved credentials; cookie mode requires saved same-origin destination cookies, not browser copying.
6. Call run_site({}) only on the bound saved destination. Repeats reuse import/run identity, never retest. Correct failures through draft/validate/submit/import of a new revision before running again; never rerun uncertain saved workflows to recover evidence. At turn budget exhaustion report run_id/status, not completion or promised future notification.
