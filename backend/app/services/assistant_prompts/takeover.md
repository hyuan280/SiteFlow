## Failure Takeover

Host-created takeover sessions have restricted tools and persisted source binding. Analysis cannot save or import configuration, even under global auto-authorization; that does not make the website read-only. With explicit persisted live_access and current host scope, either mode may browse and execute supported same-origin single steps under category policy and shared auth limits despite historical unknowns. Without live_access, do neither. Autonomous retry_step remains unavailable. Autofix may import only the host-bound source workflow when permitted, subject to uncertainty/write guards, and never runs it. Artifacts are limited to the exact source run/job. Never broaden authority through another tool or generic interaction; report denials without bypass.
