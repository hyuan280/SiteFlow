## Single Steps and Recovery

`execute_step` runs one host-authorized feature on the current original-origin page. Supply a fresh operation_id UUID and complete configuration every time; reused IDs are denied. Preconditions/step.condition are logic, not permission: isolated steps must be unconditional. Isolated post features require explicit standing policy and a bound saved site. `flow.result returns its configured boolean`; false is failure, not a reason to change it to true. Results are sanitized with bounded before/after evidence, not raw logs/secrets; the page remains open.

CAPTCHA handling does not lock the browser: continue observation, inspect, selector checks, consented screenshots, navigation, and authorized follow-up execute_step calls on the same page without reopening. Sequential CAPTCHA/login/signing tests do not inherently require a saved run. `action.fill` accepts explicit nonsecret literals only, never password, cookie, token or OTP substitutes or configuration execution. Hidden/sensitive fields, llm_prompt, and llm_enabled remain host policy.

Login sources credentials privately from the selected saved site, domain-matched Bitwarden, or bound authorized provider with credential_consent. Auth login/password-verification attempts and retries share max_auth_uses_per_turn (default 3, integer range 1-20), snapshotted per real user turn. Host reservation precedes credential resolution; failures count. Target/browser changes and internal rounds never reset it. Stop at auth_budget_exhausted until a real new user turn. `challenge.2fa` keeps OTP private and requires credential consent, but belongs to challenge and consumes no auth budget.

Historical unknown outcomes do not blanket-ban supported non-post flow/challenge/sign/action steps under current same-origin consent. This is permission, not proof of harmlessness: clicks, navigation, and page scripts may change website state. Inspect uncertain results and retained pages; never assume success/failure or require replacement first. `retry_step` accepts only the original operation_id: within host scope, admitted non-post builtins receive one-use exact-step authority and a fresh UUID without a human dialog. Auth retries consume budget; legacy approval is optional. Category policy overrides historical no_retry/requires_host_approval prose, never authorization, credentials, target, or duplicate guards. Unrelated success preserves uncertainty. Autonomous takeover denies retry_step, not all new execute_step calls; never evade exact-step restrictions.

Diagnose returned reason/lane/diagnostics, especially diagnostics.reason/evidence_errorcode; distinguish policy rejection from missing after-evidence. Historical blocked-request warnings do not prove current danger/login failure. Missing username selectors do not prove bad credentials. DNS denial means unsafe address/resolution, not proxy required; endpoint-not-approved concerns the endpoint, not site ID. page_retained=true means retained page, not evidence; secret URL denial is fatal. browser_session_unavailable/browser_response_interception_failed alone prove neither worker crash, proxy problem, nor URL violation. Missing live validation cannot exclude site/Cloudflare/credential causes; absent reason means unknown. `get_system_status` distinguishes effective Bitwarden readiness, auto-detection, sources, enabled/available/configured; an unset optional token is not unavailability. Do not reconfigure healthy detection.
