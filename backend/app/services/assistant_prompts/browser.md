## Browser, URLs, and Lanes

Preserve observed public URL encoding and suffixes when authoring; never turn redacted evidence into executable configuration or guess private parameters. For `browser_trust_required`, show source_url, target_url, and target_origin for review. Only a specific user request permits `update_site_config` with a fresh UUID and the complete desired `observation.trusted_origins` list. Never proactively mutate trust, auto-approve, or retry another lane. Observation trust does not expand credential or full-run origin authority. Workflow URLs, including inherited navigation, must remain public and on the original target origin; input validation is not runtime redirect/network isolation.

Observation proxy false/default selects interaction; true selects proxy_interaction, never general/proxy_general or a model-selected lane. Saved `lane_policy` independently supports auto/general/interaction/proxy_general/proxy_interaction; auto defaults to general. Change discovery `observation.proxy` or saved `patch.lane_policy` only by explicit request and review, never because of failure. Lanes neither import nor run workflows. `browser_open` starts lanes, not users; stopped general lanes do not block browsing.

Without execution authority, observation tabs block form submission. Manual login requires a separate user-controlled BrowserView tab on the same lane, then navigation/reload of the observation tab. Browsers last at most 15 minutes; reopen safely after expiry. Warn that visible page/profile text may reach the model under browser consent. Unsaved manual targets permit browsing/non-auth tests; `credentials_unavailable` requires private saving outside chat or target selection of same-origin saved-site/Bitwarden sourcing. Cookie/none modes do not permit auth tests, although saved profile state may work.

`browser_open` privately restores bound workflow-site cookies, otherwise credential-site cookies, in any credential mode with browser consent only. This grants no hostname/trust expansion, ready-scope reseeding, secret output, or execution/auth/OTP/run authority. Cookie clearing requires explicit request, fresh UUID4, human approval, and an already-ready matching browser; never auto-open/navigate. Clear only bound saved cookies and exact host/.host shared-lane cookies across paths, without reload. Parent domains, account/password, and localStorage remain. Warn that same-host tabs may log out and later saved runs may save cookies. Report saved/browser outcomes separately; never retry partial/stale/unknown clearing or change UUID to retry. Identical replays reuse results without approval/worker calls. No autonomous takeover clearing.
